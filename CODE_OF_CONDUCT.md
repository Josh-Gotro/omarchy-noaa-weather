# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Criticise code, not people.

## What is expected

* Be welcoming and patient, especially with people new to Omarchy, Quickshell,
  or shell scripting.
* Accept that a maintainer may decline a change without it being a judgement of
  you.
* Keep discussion on the technical question at hand.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation,
sustained disruption, or publishing someone's private information.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, commit comments, and anywhere else this
project is being represented.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately through
[a private security advisory](https://github.com/Josh-Gotro/omarchy-noaa-weather/security/advisories/new),
which is the private channel this repository has. Reports will be handled as
discreetly as the situation allows.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that violate this document,
and may block accounts for repeated or severe violations.
